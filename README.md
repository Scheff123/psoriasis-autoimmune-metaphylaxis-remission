## 🔬 Abstract  
Psoriasis is a chronic autoimmune skin disease marked by inflammation, excessive keratinocyte proliferation, and scaling. This article explores the pathogenesis of psoriasis and emphasizes the importance of metaphylaxis and the use of dermatological therapies to sustain remission. Addressing both physical and emotional burdens, the study highlights how consistent care can reduce flares and improve patient quality of life.
